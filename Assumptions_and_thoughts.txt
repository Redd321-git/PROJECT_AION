factory environment assumptions:
	1) Fixed number of machines and machine types in the factory (for both training and inference) ----> machine types :["Conveyor" ,"Robot Arm"]
	2) The modeled graph of the factory ( i.e. each machine is a node(conveyor, robotic arms etc) and the edge connecting is the flow of products) is assumed to be a DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph)
	3) The sensors in the factory are not defective, produce accurate and not null data for each valid attribute of the machine corresponding to its machine type
	4) When a machine goes under maintenance the machine regains it full health back after the maintenance
